      04 How we travel

      We plan around the light, not the landmarks.

      1. 05:00

        Arrive before the light.

        Every Solstice day is timed around the sun. We get you to the viewpoint, the boat or the trailhead while it is still dark, so a place reveals itself slowly rather than all at once.

      2. 13:00

        Leave the middle of the day empty.

        Midday light flattens everything, so we leave it unplanned: a long lunch, a swim, an hour in the shade. Nobody on a Solstice journey is hurried between sights at noon.

      3. 19:30

        Stay for the last hour.

        The final hour of light is when places are most themselves. We never schedule a transfer through it, and every itinerary ends its days outdoors.

      4. 23:00

        Let the night count, too.

        We choose lodges far enough from towns for the stars to show, and we build in nights with nothing booked except a blanket and a clear sky.
